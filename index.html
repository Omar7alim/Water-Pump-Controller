💧 Water Pump Controller

Smart Water Pump Management System

A web-based platform designed to monitor, control, and optimize water pump stations automatically. The system adjusts pump operation based on real-time water level and flow requirements, reducing human effort, energy consumption, and equipment failure.

System Features

⚙️ Automated Control

Automatically controls pump operation, speed, and power to reach the required water level efficiently.

📊 Real-Time Monitoring

Engineers can monitor water level, flow rate, power usage, and pump status through a single web interface.

⏱️ Lifetime Tracking

Tracks operating hours of each pump component and alerts users before maintenance limits are reached.

🚨 Alert System

Provides warning, maintenance, and emergency alerts to prevent failures and system overloads.

🧠 Smart Calculation

Uses both empirical mathematical methods and trial-and-error techniques to calculate required water flow accurately.

👥 Role-Based Access

Different access levels for administrators, engineers, and viewers to ensure secure and controlled system usage.
